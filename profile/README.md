## Sorplos

**Money and protection products for Nigeria, built in Lagos.**

Nigerians hold money in several banks and buy cover from several insurers, but the tools for both treat each account as if it existed alone. Sorplos builds products that work across those institutions and reach people on the channels they already use, including WhatsApp.

### What we build

**Sorplos Insure**: insurance distribution for Nigeria. Customers compare cover, verify their identity, pay and receive a verifiable digital policy certificate, on the web, in a mobile app for iOS and Android, on WhatsApp and through agents. Insurers get their own portal. Sorplos Insure is in testing while we complete licensing with NAICOM as a Partnering Insurtech, and does not sell insurance until licensed.

**Eureka**: a conversational money assistant for Nigerian bank accounts, on WhatsApp and the web. It is in beta.

### How we work

- **Regulated before launched.** A product goes out when the licensing and partnerships behind it are in place, not when the interface is ready.
- **Confirmation before consequence.** Anything that moves money or binds a policy is set out in plain language and waits for an explicit yes.
- **WhatsApp as a first-class channel**, not a fallback added to an app.

### Tech

- Backend services in **Go** (Gin) on **PostgreSQL** and **Redis**, with some **Node.js** that is being moved to Go
- Web apps in **React** with **TanStack Start**, and **Next.js**
- Mobile app in **Expo** / **React Native**
